What if everyone using Stan had a Launchpad?

Launchpad gives 30 teams 16 days with founders at their exact stage. Clan gives every creator on Stan a small group of peers like that, matched automatically and live whenever they need it. Step through the deck, or just use the phone.

Why I made this: I'm sharing the idea because I think it would be really cool if Stan built it. No ask, no strings.

Independent concept by Nitai Weinberg, inspired by Launchpad. Not an official Stan product. Every person, message and number in the phone is invented for the demo. Photos and headshots from Unsplash, used under the Unsplash License.
